Modded Options

A glimpse into what is possible for your custom modded server.

Please Note: This is not an all-inclusive list of everything we offer. It is simply a selection of the most popular options requested by server owners. If you want a plugin or mechanic not listed here, just ask us in a ticket!

🛠️ Quality of Life (QoL)

  • Kits: Custom starter, VIP, and daily kits for your players.
  • Teleportation & Home: Allow players to /tpr to friends or /home to their bases.
  • BetterLoot: Fully customized loot tables across all barrels, crates, and elite crates.
  • Backpacks: Extra inventory space with optional VIP restrictions or drop-on-death mechanics.
  • QuickSmelt & Gather Rates: Scaled gather rates (2x, 5x, 10x) and accelerated furnace smelting speeds.
  • BGrade: Allow players to automatically upgrade building blocks to wood, stone, metal, or armored as they build.

💎 VIP & Monetization

  • VIP Ranks & Permissions: Secure permission groups mapped exactly to your store tiers.
  • Queue Skip: Give your VIPs priority access when the server is full.
  • Colored Chat: Custom prefixes and name colors in chat for staff and VIPs.
  • SkinBox: Allow players to skin their items for free or lock it behind VIP access.
  • Sign Artist: Let players paste images from the internet onto signs in their base.

🎮 Gameplay Modifications

  • Raidable Bases: Automated NPC bases that spawn around the map for players to raid for loot.
  • Zombies & Custom NPC Bots: Add roaming zombies or armed scientists to monuments to increase PvE difficulty.
  • Skill Tree: An RPG-style progression system where players earn XP to unlock buffs and abilities.
  • Custom Events: Armored Train, Convoy, Space Space, Water Bases, and other premium event plugins.
  • Bradley & Heli Tiers: Modified APCs and Attack Helicopters with scaled health, damage, and loot tiers.

📊 UI & Information

  • ServerInfo Panel: A clean UI menu that shows players your rules, wipe schedule, Discord, and VIP info.
  • Custom UI: Clean on-screen displays for player count, current time, and active events.
  • Welcomer: Stylish chat or UI messages welcoming new players and displaying connection info.
  • Kill Feed: On-screen notifications or chat messages showing who killed who, with what weapon, and at what distance.
